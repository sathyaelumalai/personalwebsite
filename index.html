Mission

A Promise to My Mother

My mother managed her chronic conditions with five disconnected devices. None of them talked to each other.

i. The Witness

Ten years inside Johns Hopkins.

I spent ten years inside Johns Hopkins Hospital working in patient safety and quality outcomes. I watched my mother, Viji, try to manage diabetes, hypertension, and chronic kidney disease with five separate devices, none of which talked to each other or to her doctor.

She was the patient. She was also the integration layer. The system asked her to be both.

ii. The Promise

2024.

In 2024, I lost both my mother and my father to chronic kidney disease. My mother's last wish was simple: prevent another person from suffering as she did.

The technology I had been building for a decade arrived a few years too late for her. But, it would not arrive too late for anyone else.

iii. The Proof

Hours of warning.

The technology that arrived too late for my parents is now deployed across more than ten long-term care facilities, integrated with the major EMRs, and detecting patient decompensation in hours before today's standard of care.

It is the first FDA-cleared medical tricorder. Ten clinical-grade vitals, one device, sixty seconds. Every reading is a conversation between a patient at home or at a nursing facility and a clinician who can act in time.

Early detection is a human right.

The Device

Ten clinical-grade vitals.
Sixty seconds. Anywhere.

MouthLab is a single handheld device that captures a comprehensive health snapshot in under sixty seconds, replacing an entire drawer of monitoring tools. Dr. Gene Fridman, the co-inventor, calls it "a check engine light for humans."

A platform for the people who deliver, manage, and pay for care.

Four integrated software surfaces, built around a single longitudinal data model.

i.

Aidar Connect

For physicians

Longitudinal trends, deterioration alerts, and AIDI™ risk index, surfaced where clinical decisions are actually made. Integrates with PointClickCare and MatrixCare.

ii.

Aidar Bridge

For nursing teams

A single-screen workflow for facility nursing. Guided MouthLab readings, automatic charting, and triage queues that route the highest-risk residents to the right clinician first.

iii.

Aidar Care

For patients & families

A consumer-grade app for the home. One daily reading, plain-language guidance, and the quiet reassurance of an AI that watches over you so the people you love don't have to.

iv.

Aidar Admin

For operators & payers

Built for skilled-nursing administrators and payers. Tracks star-rating drivers, surfaces cost and reimbursement opportunities, and gives payers a clean line of sight into patient funding and outcomes.

— Journey

Two decades, one through-line.

From a B.Tech in Industrial Biotechnology in Chennai to ten years inside Johns Hopkins, to a federally funded medical device, to a billion-life mission. Drag, scroll, or use arrow keys.

  1. 2006

    Started in Chennai.

    B.Tech in Industrial Biotechnology, Anna University. The first step toward a career at the intersection of life science and engineering.

  2. 2009

    Moved to the U.S.

    Master of Science in Cell & Molecular Biology, Grand Valley State University. The science training that grounded everything that came after.

  3. 2009 – 2016

    A decade at Johns Hopkins.

    Patient safety and quality outcomes inside the Johns Hopkins Hospital system. The work that shaped the conviction.

  4. 2016

    Founded Aidar Health.

    Completed the MBA at Johns Hopkins Carey and founded what is now Aidar Health, in Columbia, Maryland.

  5. 2018

    Mom: first user.

    Viji Elumalai became the first user and tester of MouthLab. Both inspiration and proof of concept.

  6. 2019

    BARDA contract.

    Federal contract with the Biomedical Advanced Research and Development Authority for COVID-19 monitoring. AIDI™ algorithm work begins.

  7. 2021

    FDA cleared.

    MouthLab receives FDA 510(k) clearance. The first FDA-cleared medical tricorder.

  8. 2022

    Europe + Asia.

    CE Mark for Europe and MDSAP certification for global distribution. Integration partnerships with PointClickCare and MatrixCare announced.

  9. 2023

    NSF Phase II.

    $1M NSF STTR Phase II award supporting non-invasive chronic kidney disease monitoring with MouthLab.

  10. 2024

    Loss. And recognition.

    Lost both parents to chronic kidney disease within three months. Named to BBJ 40 Under 40. Briefed the bipartisan Congressional Digital Health Caucus.

  11. 2025

    DrPH at Hopkins.

    Pursuing a Doctor of Public Health at the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School. Bridging clinical innovation and population health.

  12. 2026

    10+ deployments. 1,000+ patients.

    Aidar deployed across 10+ skilled-nursing and long-term-care facilities, serving more than a thousand patients. The mission compounds.
